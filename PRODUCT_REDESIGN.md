# ALUMCONNECT — COMPLETE PRODUCT REDESIGN

## Executive Summary

Redesign and rebuild **AlumConnect** into a world-class alumni networking and career platform combining the best aspects of **LinkedIn + Google Maps + Notion + modern university alumni portals + career intelligence platform**.

**Current website**: https://alum-connect-delta.vercel.app/

---

## 1. PRODUCT VISION

### Core Positioning
> **Your alumni network, finally useful.**

### Supporting Line
> **Find the right people. Start meaningful conversations. Build your future.**

### The Problem We Solve
- Alumni directories are outdated
- Students don't know whom to approach
- Finding relevant alumni takes too much effort
- Students struggle to get genuine career guidance
- Alumni want to help but lack easy ways to find students who need them
- Colleges have alumni data but lack engaging platforms
- Networking feels transactional

### The Solution
Transform an alumni database into an **active professional community** where:
- **Discover people** → Find relevant alumni efficiently
- **Build relationships** → Create meaningful, purposeful connections
- **Find opportunities** → Access internships, jobs, referrals, mentorship
- **Ask for guidance** → Get advice from people who've done it
- **Give back** → Help the next generation succeed
- **Grow together** → Build a thriving alumni ecosystem
